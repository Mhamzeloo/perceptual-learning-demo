Session 4 · Yeotikar et al. (2013)

Peripheral Contrast Discrimination

Interactive classroom demonstration · 9 randomized trials

Instructions for participants

In each trial, you will briefly see two Gabor patterns, one above and one below the fixation cross.

  1. Keep looking at the central + throughout the trial. Do not move your eyes toward either pattern.
  2. Decide which central target looks higher in contrast (stronger dark/light stripes). Ignore the neighboring patterns (flankers).
  3. The patterns appear for 250 milliseconds. After they disappear, respond using ↑ for upper or ↓ for lower, or click the on-screen buttons.
  4. You will complete 9 trials: 3 ISO (parallel flankers), 3 CROSS (perpendicular flankers), and 3 CONTROL (no flankers), in a random order. You will not know the condition in advance.
  5. Try to answer accurately, even if unsure. Feedback appears after each response.
Important: This is a visual teaching simulation, not a calibrated psychophysical experiment. Screen size, viewing distance, contrast, and eccentricity are not controlled. The short display may not behave precisely on all devices.
